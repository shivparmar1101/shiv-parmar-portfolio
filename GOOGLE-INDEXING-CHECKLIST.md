## 📋 HOW TO REQUEST INDEXING

### Step 1: Open Google Search Console
- Go to: https://search.google.com/search-console
- Select: **shiv-parmar-portfolio.netlify.app**

### Step 2: URL Inspection
1. Click **"URL Inspection"** in left sidebar
2. Paste URL from the list above
3. Press **Enter**

### Step 3: Request Indexing
1. Click **"Request Indexing"** button
2. Wait for "Testing if live URL can be indexed" (30-60 seconds)
3. Click **"Got It"** when complete

### Step 4: Repeat
- Continue with next URL (max 10-15 per day)
- Wait for quota to reset (24 hours)
